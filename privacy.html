Privacy

Plain language, no fine print. Last updated October 2026.

The short version

Your name, your department, and your scores stay on your device. We never see them. We count anonymous activity, like "someone finished the infant CPR station," so we can improve the training and show it's working.

What stays on your device only

Everything you type (name, department, crew names, feedback drafts) and your full training record. It's saved in your browser's storage on this phone or computer. We have no copy, no account, and no way to see it. Your training record CSV and backup file go only where you send them.

What we count, anonymously

We use GoatCounter, an open-source, privacy-friendly statistics service. It uses no cookies and no tracking IDs. It records:

  • Which pages and activities were opened, finished, or left partway (and at which step).
  • Score ranges (like "80s") and attempt numbers (like "3rd try"), with no names attached.
  • General device information: browser, screen size, and approximate country and region. GoatCounter doesn't store IP addresses.
  • Which website sent you here, if any.
  • Error messages if something breaks, so we can fix it.

We use these numbers to find where training is confusing, fix bugs, and report results to grant funders, always as totals, never about any one person.

What we never do

No ads. No selling or sharing data. No cookies. No accounts or passwords. No collecting names, emails, or anything you type into the app.

Turn off statistics on this device

One switch covers every module on this device.

Who runs Preconnect

Preconnect is a free, nonprofit project built by firefighters in Bay County, Michigan. Questions or concerns? Use the feedback page.
