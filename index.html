Preconnect is a free, independent training platform made in Bay County, Michigan. A preconnect is the hoseline that's hooked up and ready before the call, so it flows the moment you need it. That's what practice does for skills. Preconnect is practice: it doesn't replace hands-on training, your department's protocols, or official certification courses. Privacy
