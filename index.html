ABOUT

Preconnect is a free training platform built in Bay County, Michigan. A preconnect is the hoseline that's hooked up and ready before the call, so it flows the moment you need it. That's what practice does for skills.

Preconnect is practice. It doesn't replace hands-on training, your department's protocols, or official certification courses.
