    Charge the Line

    Every call starts at the panel.

    Modeled on 10-2: HME rescue pumper, Hale Q-Flo 1250 GPM, 800-gallon tank, FRC pressure governor, Hale MIV electric master intake valve.

    Crack opens a valve a quarter turn at a time. Snap throws it wide open — water hammer, or a lost prime at draft. The MIV travels on its own; that's what it's for.

    Three tiers per scenario: Guided shows every step and the target pressure. Recall hides the checklist and makes you figure the PDP from the hose layout. Chaos is Recall plus things going wrong.

    Training aid only. Department SOPs and the preconnect chart on the rig are the real numbers.
